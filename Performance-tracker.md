3. Calculated Metrics

Use the same measurement period when comparing videos.

- Instagram share rate = Shares ÷ Accounts reached × 100
- Instagram save rate = Saves ÷ Accounts reached × 100
- Instagram like rate = Likes ÷ Accounts reached × 100
- YouTube like rate = Likes ÷ Views × 100
- YouTube share rate = Shares ÷ Views × 100

If the denominator is zero or unavailable, mark the result as N/A.

6. Data Rules

1. Never invent or estimate missing analytics.
2. Record the measurement date and platform.
3. Compare videos of similar age and duration when possible.
4. Separate observed facts from hypotheses.
5. Do not call a video successful based on views alone.
6. Review the 24-hour and 7-day snapshots separately.
7. Use platform-provided analytics; metric definitions may differ between platforms.
